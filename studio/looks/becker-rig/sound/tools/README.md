# Becker Rig sound pack: calibration tools

The pack levels its cues and sets its music against values fitted on the 15 Becker teasers' cue lists. When a kit or a spec moves a cue, those values describe a teaser that no longer exists. `test/cues.test.mjs` then fails with `cue list changed for <id>: refit the sound pack, see looks/becker-rig/sound/tools/README.md`, which points here.

Run everything from `studio/`. The tools read the cue records from `--cues <file>` (or `--cues=<file>`; written by `node src/cli.mjs cues specs/*.json --out <file>`). Without `--cues` they read `test/fixtures/cues.json`, the records `MODEL` was fitted on, and say so on stderr. After a kit or spec change that fixture still holds the old cue lists until step 3 of BIBLE.md §11.1, so a refit must pass a fresh dump. An unknown flag stops the tool. Id prefixes on the command line (`09b 10b`) limit a run to those teasers.

## What is fitted, and what is designed

| Value | Where | How it is set |
|---|---|---|
| `MODEL.C`, `MODEL.tiltUp`, `MODEL.tiltDn` | `balance.mjs` | Fitted by `fit.mjs` + `fitsolve.py`: the music stem before the trim, `M = C + duck cost + tiltUp·max(0, tilt) + tiltDn·max(0, -tilt)`. `tiltDn` (2026-10-13) is the loudness the bright side of the tilt adds (the toys up by \|tilt\| dB); without it 02a's and 08c's music came out 0.7-0.8 dB over the prediction and the residual's spread was 0.42 dB, with it 0.27 |
| `MODEL.a`, `MODEL.b` | `balance.mjs` | Fitted the same way: the master-gain prediction, `mix power = (1 + a)·M + b·U`. Since 2026-10-13 `b` is fitted against the plan's own U (`Upred`, the value `musicTrim` feeds it), not the engine's: fitting on the engine's U and feeding it the plan's biased every predicted gain (05b, 06c, 03a 0.7-0.9 dB), so the cards came out too loud (B8). The card (its SFX and the logo) is trimmed against the predicted gain |
| `MODEL.iBias` | `balance.mjs` | Fitted by `fitsolve.py` on the delivered render: the mean of the engine's gated SFX stem minus the plan's own render of it. The plan renders every cue as the engine will (the ring budget included) and, since 2026-10-13, places the music twice: once to get the card trim, then again with the card's cues trimmed by it, as `sfx.mjs` plays them. Before that the card's trim (-1.5 to -0.75 dB on its cues) was most of the bias (-0.42 to -0.58 LU, and 0.5-1.7 LU in a short body, where the card's cues are a large share of the stem); now it reads +0.02 LU, sd 0.10, and holds only what the merges change. There is no designed exception: nothing the engine plays is left out of the plan |
| `MODEL.dIS`, `dISTilt`, `dISTiltMax`, `trimLo`, `trimHi` | `balance.mjs` | Designed: the music sits `dIS` LU under the gated SFX, up to `dISTiltMax` dB nearer for a strongly tilted SFX spectrum or a crowded 1 kHz octave (`CROWD`); the trim is clamped to ±3 dB |
| `CROWD.ref`, `CROWD.refShort`, `CROWD.k` | `balance.mjs` | Designed (2026-10-12, 2026-10-13): the reference-octave crowding. `crowd` is the cues' long-term 1 kHz-octave power over the body re their gated loudness (the plan's SFX render with the leveller's trims, before the card's cues are trimmed: `l1k` is read over [0, base], so the card must not move its denominator; with it in, a card 1.5 dB down moved 10b's music 0.5 dB nearer). Above the reference the music comes `k` (1.0) dB nearer per dB, taking the larger of this and the tilt term, under the same 2 dB cap. The reference is 54.5 for a body of 25 s or more (it bit only where bells owned the octave every §9.2 band is measured against: 06c, 07c, 09b) and falls to `refShort` 53 at 12.5 s and under (`crowdRef`, by `sfx.mjs` `shortness`): a short body puts more bells per second into that octave. Keyed on the body's length so no long shipped teaser moves (07b, 08b, 09a, 09b, 10b read as before). On the fresh dump the short cuts read 01b 55.1, 01c 54.6, 03a 54.9, 04b 54.3. Without the short reference, the TENSION octave pump alone keeps their 250 Hz octave inside (01b 0.6, 01c 0.9 dB), but 03a's 125 Hz and 8 kHz octaves and its limiter (A11 2.6%, target 2.5) need the nearer band, so it stays. Not touched by a refit: `fit.mjs` renders at a fixed trim |
| `MODEL.gRef`, `MODEL.cardMax`, `CARD` | `balance.mjs` | Designed: the card trim, `card = clamp(gRef - predicted gain, -1.5, cardMax)`. With the card in its linear range the logo lands at about `gRef - 17.7` LUFS momentary (B8's M(L+0.2); `CARD.logo` -17.7, ±0.12 over the 15, -0.33 on 04b), -12.0 at 5.7. Until 2026-10-13 the card went up to +1.0 for a low predicted gain; a dense body (low gain) then put the sting over a payoff that the same low gain had lowered. `cardMax` is -0.75. In a dense body (the leveller short of `SFX_TARGET`) the card is also capped so the logo stays `CARD.gap` (0.5) dB under the payoff's planned SFX momentary (`pMom`, the plan's SFX stem at P + 0.2 s, which the band only adds to), and never under `CARD.floor` (-12.5 LUFS: B8's -13 plus a gated check's 0.5; the gain prediction's error, sd 0.3 dB, comes out of it). The B8 payoff window is where the encode loop dips: the decoded payoff goes over the PLR limit and the loop lowers ±25 ms around it, 0.5-2 dB, unpredictably (a 0.1 dB change elsewhere moves it). Changing any of these moves cards: check B8 in `verify` |
| `PAYOFF_LIFT` | `balance.mjs` | Designed (2026-10-13): when the leveller cannot bring the SFX stem down to `SFX_TARGET` (its caps protect the cues B3 listens to), the payoff's own cues (P - 0.3 .. P + 0.35 s) stand up by the excess, at most 1 dB, so the payoff keeps its height over a dense body (05b 0.41, 04b 0.22 dB on the fresh dump). Without it 05b's payoff fell to its sting (B8 0.00) |
| `OWN`, `PAYOFF_OWN` | `balance.mjs` (used by `compose.mjs`) | Designed (2026-10-13): the 50 ms a cue B3 listens to owns (bible §9.3 B3), and the 1.2 s after P in which such a cue is the payoff's own: the fanfare's "TA" dips under it, and, once the payoff duck's hold is over, the band's mid voices and its crash, each for that cue's 50 ms (`DIP_P`). The whole band is never ducked for it, and `duckCost` models only the engine's ducks |
| `RING`, `shortness` | `sfx.mjs` | Designed (2026-10-13): every ringing layer the cues bring rings τ × `ring` = clamp(base / 25, 0.3, 1): a body ding's glock (and its level and hall send, 10·log10 ring dB), the payoff's ding (half that level step), and every big hit's crash, the payoff's included (and darker: LP 7.5 kHz × ring). 25 s is the median body the pack's levels were set on, so bodies of 25 s and over ring as shipped. `shortness(base)` = clamp((25 - base) / 12.5, 0, 1) keys the other short-body rules (the TENSION pump, `crowdRef`). Visible to the plan. See the look README, "Short and dense bodies" |
| `CLIMB`, `LOW`, `SPREAD`, `TEN` | `compose.mjs` | Designed (2026-10-13): the hook settle and the BUILD crescendo, a BUILD shorter than B7's window (step, head, the TENSION climbing on, dropLift) and a HOLD shorter than it (holdDip) (B7); the bass's dull lift yielding to the cues' landings (§9.2 125 Hz); the velocity-spread guard (S8, by construction); the TENSION walk's octave pump by body length and its lead's share of a short body (§9.2 250 Hz and 1 kHz). The trim pays for every level the music model does not see (`shapeDb`). See the look README |
| `SFX_TARGET`, `RANGE`, `MAX_TRIM`, `MAX_TRIM_AUDIBLE` | `balance.mjs` | Designed: the SFX leveller (the gated SFX stem pinned to -22.9 LUFS pre-master, riding only the top 14 dB of moments, at most 6 dB, 3 dB for a cue B3 listens to) |
| `TILT_REF` and the tilt slopes | `balance.mjs`, `compose.mjs` | Chosen with the level optimiser (`opt.py`) |
| Voice levels | `mix.mjs` | Chosen with the level optimiser, then by ear |

## Refit `MODEL` (after any change to levels, sends, ducks, the arrangement, the SFX renderers, or a cue list)

The same commands as BIBLE.md §11.2:

```bash
node src/cli.mjs cues specs/*-becker-rig-*.json --out /tmp/snd-br/cues.json                 # today's records: the fixture may hold the old ones
node looks/becker-rig/sound/tools/fit.mjs --cues /tmp/snd-br/cues.json > /tmp/fit.jsonl     # every Becker teaser at trim 0 (about 3.5 min; split by id prefixes to run in parallel)
python3 looks/becker-rig/sound/tools/fitsolve.py /tmp/fit.jsonl                              # needs numpy: prints a, b, C, tiltUp, tiltDn, iBias and the residuals
```

1. Copy the printed `a`, `b`, `C`, `tiltUp`, `tiltDn` (and `iBias`) into `MODEL` in `balance.mjs`. This changes the audio of every Becker teaser.
2. Check the prediction: `node looks/becker-rig/sound/tools/trims.mjs --cues /tmp/snd-br/cues.json` prints each plan's predicted master gain (`gain`); compare it with `master.gainDb` in the build's `<id>.report.json`.
3. Run `npm run sound:verify`. It prints a `CUE DRIFT` block until the fixture is updated; that is expected here.
4. When everything passes, finish with BIBLE.md §11.1 steps 3-7: store the records (`node src/cli.mjs cues specs/*.json --out test/fixtures/cues.json`), regenerate the tempo table (`node sound/tools/tempo-table.mjs --write`), update Appendix A's pins if 09b, 02a or 10b moved, `npm test`, record the change, then ship (§11.5, which rewrites the baseline). Say in the commit which masters changed.

**State on 2026-10-13, refit and shipped (BIBLE v2.4).** Seven condensed cuts (01b, 01c, 02a, 03a, 04b, 05a, 05b) moved their cue records, and the pack gained the short- and dense-body rules (look README, "Short and dense bodies"), so `MODEL` was refit on a fresh dump of the 15 records. The fit changed in three ways, all in `fit.mjs` / `fitsolve.py` / `balancePlan`: the music model has a `tiltDn` term (the toys' bright lift), `b` is fitted against the plan's own U, and the plan renders and places with the card's cues trimmed as they will sound, so `iBias` is fitted on the delivered render with nothing left out (+0.02 LU, sd 0.10; it was -0.42 to -0.58, mostly the unmodelled card trim). After the last level rule (the payoff punch, the TENSION share, the body-I crowding), round 8 gave C -23.66, tiltUp 0.88, tiltDn 0.31, a 0.074, b 1.140, iBias 0.02, which `MODEL` now holds; round 9 with them gave a 0.073, b 1.142 and the rest unchanged, so this is the fixed point. The two rules added after it touch one teaser each and go through the trim (09b's eased DROP, `shapeDb`) or the card only (05b, `CARD.floor`). The music-stem residual is -0.59 to +0.61 dB (sd 0.32), the master-gain fit error -0.17 to +0.17 dB at trim 0, and against the verify build the plan's predicted gain is off by -0.41 (02a) to +0.53 dB (03a), mean +0.12. `verify --detail` on the fresh dump (`/tmp/snd-short-becker-rig/after.txt` in the sound pass): the 15 Becker teasers pass every gated check; see the look README for the margins. §11.1 steps 3-7 and the ship (§11.5) followed the same day: the records are stored, the tempo table regenerated (02a's thud train is gone, so the test pins it as none), `npm test` passes 28/28, and all 15 Becker masters changed against v2.3, byte-identical to that verify build.

**State on 2026-10-12, refit and shipped.** 06c's condensed cut moved its cue record, so `MODEL` was refit on a fresh dump of the 15 records, as above: two rounds, because the card trim, and so the fitted mix, reads the predicted gain. Round 1 from the stored values gave C -23.37, a 0.065, b 1.159, tiltUp 0.63, iBias -0.41; round 2 gave a 0.061, b 1.165, iBias -0.42 (C and tiltUp unchanged), which `MODEL` now holds; a third round moved a by -0.002, b by +0.003 and tiltUp by +0.01, under 0.01 dB of predicted gain, so this is the fixed point. The fit's master-gain error is -0.19 to +0.09 dB at trim 0, but its music-stem residual (`resid`) stays -0.59 to +0.61 dB, which is what `C + cost + tiltUp·tilt` cannot model. So against the shipped build's reports, `trims.mjs`'s predicted gain is still off by -0.66 (06c) to +0.37 dB (02a), mean -0.20, about what the stale values gave (-0.54 to +0.43, mean -0.18). The refit puts the coefficients on today's pack and records, but it cannot remove the music model's residual. Every teaser passes B8. The values it replaced (-23.46, 0.007, 1.288, 0.88, -0.35) were fitted before the last pack fixes. The same change added the reference-octave crowding (`CROWD`, above) for 06c's 250 Hz octave. Both went out with the ship of 2026-10-12 (BIBLE.md §11.5), together with the engine's AAC pre-roll. The tightest BANDS margins after that ship (`verify`, re-measured after 06c's picture-only re-ship the same day) are 02a 2 kHz 0.1 dB, 04b 250 Hz 0.1, 09a 2 kHz 0.1 and 01c 500 Hz 0.2; next come 05b 8 kHz 0.3 and 01b 8 kHz, 05a 500 Hz and 08c 250 Hz at 0.5. The previous build's MP4s had the same margins. They will flip first on the next pack change or refit, so check them with `verify --detail` (and `bandparts.mjs <id>`) first, and widen them with `opt.py` when there is room.

## Levels: the optimiser

```bash
node looks/becker-rig/sound/tools/dump.mjs > /tmp/dump.jsonl        # per instrument: octave bands, BUILD loudness and 500-1200 Hz power (about 5 min)
python3 looks/becker-rig/sound/tools/opt.py /tmp/dump.jsonl         # needs numpy: a random search, then a local refine
```

`opt.py` searches per-voice level offsets and the tilt slopes. It keeps every teaser's §9.2 bands inside the Becker windows with the most margin, while the marimba stays 0.4 LU above the MALLET floor and the loudest voice in 500-1200 Hz. It reads the windows and the floor from `sound/acceptance.json`. The result is a proposal: copy it into `mix.mjs` / `compose.mjs` by hand, then refit `MODEL` (above).

## Diagnostics

| Tool | What it prints |
|---|---|
| `trims.mjs [ids]` | The balance plan per teaser, without rendering audio: the leveller (I0, I, threshold, rides per kind), U, the tilt, the music plan (cost, M, trim, predicted gain, card trim) |
| `bandparts.mjs <id>` | Where each §9.2 octave's energy comes from: the mix, the stems and every instrument, in dB re the mix's 1 kHz octave. Run it first when BANDS fails |

`common.mjs` holds the shared plumbing. The per-instrument re-render (`instrumentBuses`) is shared with the MALLET check in `sound/checks/looks/becker-rig.mjs`.

## History

These tools were the fixer's scratchpad scripts (2026-10-11), kept raw in `sound/tools/_preserved/` and folded in here with repo-relative paths. The acceptance scripts (`check.mjs`, `fixcheck.mjs`, `ucheck.mjs`) became `sound/checks/`. These one-off investigations were dropped:
- `kb.mjs`: the kick + bass true-peak study.
- `devsum.py`: a summary of a lost script's output.
- `evalp.py`: a variant evaluator that exec'd `opt.py`.
- `retparts.mjs`: the returns breakdown, now covered by B1's returns bound and INFO's DUCKSTATS.
- `sfxbands.mjs`: SFX bands per kind (the `sfx` row of `bandparts.mjs` covers the stem).
