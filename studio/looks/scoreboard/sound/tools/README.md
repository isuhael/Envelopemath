# Scoreboard sound pack: calibration

The pack's SFX fader, its opening lift, its band guard and the sting's level are models fitted on the 15 Scoreboard teasers. When a kit or a spec moves a cue, the fitted values describe teasers that no longer exist. `test/cues.test.mjs` then fails with `cue list changed for <id>: refit the sound pack, see looks/scoreboard/sound/tools/README.md`, which points here.

## The values

`calibration.json` (next to the pack's modules) holds the fitted values. `cal.mjs` reads it once at load time; when the file is missing, `cal.mjs` uses defaults measured on 2026-10-11. `tools/calibrate.mjs` measures the values from a full render with stems.

| Value | Used by | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `BEDS` | `balance.mjs` (the fader's music model), `compose.mjs` (the opening lift), `sfx.mjs` `bedSec` | The music's power over whole sections, by section name and energy tier, pre-master, at the bus's 0 dB. It is measured on `music.dry` (before the event duck) with the energy curve (`energy.mjs`) taken out. A teaser whose bed gave way (a fader offset) is left out |
| `M_FIT` | `balance.mjs`, `compose.mjs` | The music-stem model's residual: the measured music stem minus (BEDS + energy curve under the ducks + OFF_EFF × offset), averaged over the teasers |
| `GM` | `compose.mjs` (opening lift) | The master gain plus the measured music stem (pre-master). The master gain then follows as `GM - M` |
| `GM_PLAN` | `logo.mjs` | The master gain plus the plan's own music estimate. With `GM_SFX` it predicts the master gain the sting's scale is set from, so the sting lands at its aim after mastering (B8) |
| `LOGO_PRE` | `logo.mjs` | The sting's momentary loudness in the 400 ms window ending exactly at L + 0.2 s, pre-master, at scale 1 (on the delivered AAC): the sting's true level, whatever the meter's phase |
| `OPEN_FIT` | `compose.mjs` | The delivered short-term loudness at 3 s minus the opening lift's prediction (which leaves out the returns, the ducks and the glue). Each `--write` adds the new mean residual to the current value |
| `GM_SFX` | `logo.mjs` (`gainEst`) | The cues' weight in the master gain model: G = `GM_PLAN` − 10 log10(10^(M/10) + `GM_SFX` · 10^(Su/10)), M the plan's music estimate (plus the band guard's loudness change), Su the cues' K-weighted power over the body at their final level (`sfx.mjs` `sfxBodyPower`). Fitted with `GM_PLAN` (least spread). With the music alone a short, busy body mastered 0.4-0.9 dB under the estimate |
| `LOGO_SLOPE` | `logo.mjs` (`logoPlan`) | The sting's rise at L + 0.2 (dB/s, about 15). B8 reads the meter's 100 ms hop nearest L + 0.2 (up to 50 ms off), so its reading is the true level plus `LOGO_SLOPE` × `b8Frame(L)`; the meter guard moves the sting only where that reading would sit less than 0.5 dB inside the teaser's B8 window |
| `BAND_REF` | `bands.mjs` (`predict`) | `{ w }`: the cues' (and their returns') weight against the music in the band model, for the SFX bus compressor and HP, fitted on the 63-250 Hz octaves and used on every octave. Until 2026-10-13 the 8 and 16 kHz octaves had a weight of their own, `w16`, fitted on the 16 kHz octave; it had no fixed point (see the refit for the condensed 07a-10c below), so it is gone (`predict` still takes one when a probe passes it, and `calibrate.mjs --per` prints what a free one would gain) |
| `BAND_FIT` | `bands.mjs` | The band model's residual per octave (delivered minus predicted, dB re 1 kHz, mean over the teasers): the SFX bus compressor, the master's filters and limiter, the AAC, the cues' cross terms with the music. Since 2026-10-13 the model runs the glue, leaves the react and logo buses unducked (as the engine does) and sums the groups with their cross terms (`crossOctaves`), so the fit is small: within 0.45 dB on every octave but 31.5 Hz |
| `BAND_SD` | `bands.mjs` (`bandAim`) | The residual's spread per octave. The band guard aims each watched octave 0.5 + 3 × `BAND_SD` dB inside its window. At 16 kHz most of it is the AAC encoder, not the model: on two renders of near-identical masters the master WAV's 16 kHz octave moved by 0.11 dB at most while the delivered AAC's moved by up to 0.68 dB (03c: −14.74 and −14.72 dB re 1 kHz on the WAV, −14.40 and −15.08 on the AAC), so a 15-teaser estimate of that spread reads 0.20-0.28 dB from render to render |
| `PAY_LOSS`, `PAY_HIT` | `logo.mjs` (`payoffEst`) | The payoff's delivered momentary loudness in B8's frame against the band model's payoff frame plus the master gain: what every payoff loses (about 0.3 dB: the AAC, the master's filters), and how much more when a hit marks P (about 1.5 dB: the SFX bus compressor's 2.5 dB on the loudest hit, the limiter at P). Spread 0.29 dB (`stats.PAY_sd`; 0.39 at v2.4, 0.53 while the model ducked the stab at P). The sting aims at the middle of [-13 LUFS, the payoff] when that is under `LOGO_AT`, and the meter guard keeps the spread (`b8Spread`: 0.14 dB at the floor, kept `B8_K` = 3 times; 0.32 against the payoff) inside both edges. The payoff's reading also moves with the encode loop: a local dip it puts on the payoff transient takes 0.1-0.2 dB off it (04a, BIBLE §11.6 item 4) |
| `RANGE` | `bands.mjs` (`rangeEst`), `compose.mjs` (the DROP's range lift) | The range model: `body`, the delivered body's 100 ms K-weighted blocks against the band model's (`mixBlocks`) at the master gain estimate (the median offset, mean over the teasers, about −0.2 dB); `card`, the card's 25 blocks against the sting's true level at L + 0.2 (mean per block: the card is the same in every teaser); `fit` and `sd`, the delivered A3 against `rangeEst` as compose() runs it (the payoff window limited as the master limits it, `payoffBurst`), mean residual and spread (−0.03 and 0.17 LU over the 15). The DROP's range lift aims `energy.mjs` `RANGE.margin` + `RANGE.k` × `sd` over A3's floor |
| `OFF_EFF` | `balance.mjs` | How far the music stem moves per dB of bed offset (0.65). It was measured by hand on 02c at offsets of -1.3 and -2.5 dB (0.63-0.70). `calibrate.mjs` passes it through unchanged |
| `stats` | (information) | The spread of each fit over the teasers, the teaser count and the render it came from |

**BEDC** (the music's 50 ms RMS at the cue times) is retired: `clearance.mjs` renders the bed at every cue window instead, so no BEDC value exists or needs fitting.

**Designed constants** are not fitted; change them deliberately. In `balance.mjs`:
- `S_MINUS_M` -1.35: a busy teaser cuts its cues until the SFX stem sits 1.35 LU under the music stem.
- `S_FLOOR` -2.1: a sparse teaser lifts its cues only until the SFX stem is 2.1 LU under the music stem.
- `LIFT_MAX` 4: the largest cue lift, in dB.
- `S_CAL` -0.3: what the SFX bus takes off the solo renders.

In `logo.mjs`, `LOGO_AT` -12.4 is the sting's true momentary loudness at L + 0.2 (the 400 ms window ending exactly there), 0.6 dB inside B8's floor; `B8_MARGIN` 0.5 is what the meter guard keeps inside each edge of B8's window, plus the estimates' spread. In `bands.mjs`, `BAND_MARGIN` 0.5 and `BAND_K` 3 set the guard's aim, `LEVER_RANGE`, `REVOICE_MAX` 1.5, `MID_UP_MAX` 3 (how far the mid layers may come up for the 2 kHz floor, step 5 of `guard`) and `LEVER_DM_MAX` 0.25 (0.5 for a lifted teaser the fader follows, `leverDmMax`) its levers. In `energy.mjs`, `SHORT_BUILD` and `B7_MARGIN` 0.6 size the short BUILD's climb and ease, `EARLY_P` the early payoff's drop-then-breakdown, and `RANGE` the DROP's range lift (`margin` 0.5 and `k` 1: the lift aims A3's floor + 0.5 + one `CAL.RANGE.sd`; `maxDb` 2, the most it lifts). In `compose.mjs`, `PAY_ROOM.maxDb` 2 is the most the hit at P comes up for B8's room, and `PAY_BURST` the payoff's limiter budget (`pct` 1.5: the predicted burst kept under 1.5% of the file, half A11's gate; the hit's hall down first, in `hallStep` 0.5 dB steps to `hallDb` −6, then the hit in `step` 0.1 dB steps to `maxDb` −3). In `logo.mjs`, `B8_K` 3: the meter guard keeps three of the floor estimate's spreads inside B8's floor (one against the payoff). The band guard's floors are 63, 125, 250, 500 Hz and 2 kHz (`bands.mjs` `guard`). The B1 windows the fader aims at are in `sound/acceptance.json`.

**Thresholds the pack reads at render time.** Three `sound/acceptance.json` values move the masters, not only the verdicts (`cal.mjs` `acceptance()`): the BANDS windows (`bands.mjs`, the band guard aims inside them), B8's `minLufs` (`logo.mjs`, the sting and the hit at P's room) and, since 2026-10-13, A3's Scoreboard floor (`checks.A3.looks.scoreboard.min`, `compose.mjs` `rangeClimb`: the DROP's range lift aims `RANGE.margin` + `RANGE.k` × `CAL.RANGE.sd` over it). An edit to any of them is a pack change: verify all 15, refit when the fitted values move, and rewrite the baseline (BIBLE §9.0, §11.4).

## Refit (after any change to the arrangement, the levels, the sends, the duck table or a cue list)

The same commands as BIBLE.md §11.2. Dump fresh records first: after a kit or spec change, `test/fixtures/cues.json` still holds the old cue lists.

```bash
cd studio
node src/cli.mjs cues specs/*-scoreboard-*.json --out /tmp/snd-sb/cues.json
node src/cli.mjs audio specs/*-scoreboard-*.json --cues /tmp/snd-sb/cues.json --out /tmp/snd-sb/run --stems --no-check
node looks/scoreboard/sound/tools/calibrate.mjs /tmp/snd-sb/run --cues /tmp/snd-sb/cues.json            # prints the values
node looks/scoreboard/sound/tools/calibrate.mjs /tmp/snd-sb/run --cues /tmp/snd-sb/cues.json --write    # stores them
```

`calibrate.mjs` needs `--cues` (the records the render was made from; `--cues=<file>` works too), takes `--skip <id prefixes>`, `--per` (each teaser's residuals on stderr) and `--write`, and stops on any other flag. The render takes about 1 GB in `/tmp`.

Calibrate a render with the calibration that made it still loaded: `calibrate.mjs` composes every teaser again to read its plan, its band model and its levers, and the band guard's levers depend on the calibration. A code change between the render and the calibration (or a `--write` in between) gives residuals for levers the render did not use.

Each calibration moves the next render a little: the fader, the lift and the sting read the tables. So render again and calibrate again, until the printed values stop moving (two rounds are usually enough). Run without `--write` to check the state. Then:
1. Run `npm run sound:verify`. It prints a `CUE DRIFT` block until the fixture is updated; that is expected here.
2. When it passes, finish with BIBLE.md §11.1 steps 3-7: store the records (`node src/cli.mjs cues specs/*.json --out test/fixtures/cues.json`), regenerate the tempo table (`node sound/tools/tempo-table.mjs --write`), update Appendix A's pins if 09c or 10a moved, `npm test`, record the change, then ship (§11.5, which rewrites the baseline).
3. Say in the commit which masters changed.

**Refit for the condensed 07a-10c (2026-10-13, after BIBLE v2.4).** The five Scoreboard cuts promoted after v2.4 (07a 08a 09c 10a 10c) moved five records, and the pack gained five rules (the DROP's range lift, the hit at P's level, the band guard's 2 kHz floor and its mid-layer step, `B8_K`) and two model parts: `RANGE` (new) and the band model's payoff window (`payoffBurst`, which feeds the hit's budget). `calibrate.mjs` fits `RANGE`, counts the range lift in the fader's music model (`dMrange` in `fits.dM`) and, since the fixer's round, fits one cue weight for every octave. Refitted from a fresh dump (`--cues`) by the loop above:
- *The designer's rounds.* Round 1 on the v2.4 code (written: GM −16.55, GM_PLAN −15.75, GM_SFX 0.33, OPEN_FIT 2.10, PAY_HIT 1.48, `BAND_REF.w16` 0.96) alone left 08a's A11 at 3.08% (gate 3%) and 09c's LRA at 4.00. Then the rules, then round 2 with them (written, with `RANGE`: GM −16.52, GM_PLAN −15.78, GM_SFX 0.29, M_FIT 1.67, OPEN_FIT 2.08, PAY_LOSS 0.32, PAY_HIT 1.49, `RANGE` body −0.22, fit −0.03, sd 0.17). The checks after it moved the scalars by 0.03 dB at most, BEDS not at all, `BAND_SD` by 0.01 and `RANGE` by 0.01, but `BAND_REF.w16` and `BAND_FIT` 8000/16000 went 0.16-0.36 dB from round to round, in both directions (w16 1.11 → 1.13 → 0.98 → 0.95).
- *Why w16 had no fixed point.* The cues' share of the 8 and 16 kHz octaves is about the same in every teaser, so a change of w16 moves every teaser's 16 kHz prediction alike and `BAND_FIT` absorbs it. On the designer's final build the 16 kHz spread was 0.214 dB at `w` (1.01), 0.211 at the best free weight (0.95), and within 0.01 dB of that anywhere in 0.84-1.06 (`calibrate.mjs --per` prints this line). So `w16` is gone, and `w` serves every octave.
- *The fixer's rounds.* Only the band model was written, twice: on the designer's final build (`BAND_REF` {w 1.01}, `BAND_FIT` 8000 −0.19 and 16000 −0.29, `BAND_SD` 16000 0.21), then on the verify build with the guard's 2 kHz step (`BAND_FIT` 250 0.05, 2000 0.09, 8000 −0.20, 16000 −0.34, 31.5 −2.08; `BAND_SD` 500 0.18, 4000 0.15, 8000 0.21, 16000 0.27). The check on the next verify build (the one shipped) moved `w` not at all (the free top-octave weight 1.04, within 0.01 dB of the least spread anywhere in 0.92-1.17), `BAND_FIT` by 0.02 at most but 16 kHz by 0.04, `BAND_SD` by 0.01 at most but 16 kHz by 0.03 (0.27 → 0.24): the band model sits at its fixed point.
- *What still moves at 16 kHz is the AAC encoder, not the model.* Between two renders of near-identical masters the master WAV's 16 kHz octave moved by 0.11 dB at most, the delivered AAC's by up to 0.68 dB (03c: −14.74 and −14.72 dB re 1 kHz on the WAV, −14.40 and −15.08 on the AAC). So `BAND_FIT[16000]` and `BAND_SD[16000]` carry the encoder's top octave, and a 15-teaser estimate of that spread reads 0.21-0.28 dB from render to render.
- *The scalars were not written in the fixer's rounds.* On four builds they read M_FIT 1.70-1.71 (written 1.67), GM_PLAN −15.77 to −15.71 with GM_SFX 0.30-0.33 (−15.78 and 0.29; the near-degenerate pair moves the predicted master gain by 0.03 dB at most), PAY_HIT 1.52-1.55 (1.49), OPEN_FIT 2.05 (2.08), LOGO_PRE −16.77 to −16.78 (−16.76), BEDS by 0.1 in two cells (HOOK 3, CADENCE 3) and `RANGE` by 0.02. Each is 0.03-0.05 dB from the written value, a sixth of its own spread or less. A trial write of all of them (one more verify build) passed every gate and moved no margin that mattered, except 02b's 16 kHz octave, which went from 1.11 to 0.57 dB inside its ceiling: the WAV moved 0.15 dB, the AAC's top octave 0.38. So the written scalars stay, and the next refit should write M_FIT and PAY_HIT (their shift has kept its sign on every build; PAY_HIT moves with the encode loop's local dips at P, BIBLE §11.6 item 4).
- The written values have these spreads over the 15: GM_PLAN 0.13 dB, LOGO_PRE 0.04, the payoff 0.29, the range 0.17 LU, the bands 0.18-0.27 on the watched octaves. 05c's 0.3 dB at 16 kHz on an intermediate render was the hit's crash layer raised after the air shelves were set, not the fit: `airShelves` now runs again once the hit has its level.
