## 0. The short version

1. **Two looks, one rule: no cuts.** All four watched reels are a single continuous shot, 0 cuts per 10 s. The motion comes from a pointer (a pen or stylus on the notebook sheet, or a hand, pen or cash stack in the talking head) and from text that pops in or swaps.
2. **The two biggest reels show the whole finished sheet at frame 1.** "The Paycheck Rule That Changed My Finances" (739.3K) and "3 PAYCHECK RULES" (142.8K) both open on a complete, dense, colour-coded cheat sheet. The progressively revealed "How Long Will it Take to Save $100,000 making $X/hr?" series, whose covers show the page mostly blank, sits at 6.7K to 28.9K. Only 12 reels were sampled, and the series posts are newer, so treat this as a signal, not proof.
3. **The number device is "formula, then result".** In the talking-head format a typed line such as `5,000 X 200` or `$2,600 x .40` appears next to an empty list slot and is then replaced by the answer (`$1M`, `$1,040/Month`). On the notebook sheets the formula is written small and grey, and the result sits in a green highlighter box (`$25/hr x 40 hrs/week = $1,000/week`).
4. **Every example runs on one round anchor input:** a $5,000 monthly income or paycheck (5 of the 12 reels, counting the couple's combined $5,000), or $20, $25 or $30 an hour (6 of the 12).
5. **The badge formula is "$INPUT/HR → $OUTPUT"**: a black rounded pill with white bold sans-serif text across the middle of the frame (`$20/HR → $10K SAVED`, `$25/HR → $100K`, `$20/HR → $100K?! 🤯`).
6. **The open loop is an empty numbered list.** `1: 2: 3: 4: 5:` sits on screen with nothing filled in, under "Do all 5 and watch your finance change". The viewer can see how many payoffs are still to come.
7. **The two biggest reels have no comment-keyword CTA.** The 739.3K reel's caption opens with a personal story and the 115.8K reel's with a question. The "Comment TEMPLATE" or "Comment INCOME" reels range from 6.7K to 50.2K. Again, the sample is small.

## 2. Look profile: the recurring visual system

### Format A: "Notebook sheet + pointer" (faceless; 7 of 12 reels)
- **Framing:** top-down. A portrait page (spiral-bound or ring-punched, lined, off-white or cream) fills about 85 to 90% of the 9:16 frame, with a dark border around it (a dark desk or a tablet bezel; the $50K cover shows a dark tablet-like frame). In the 739K reel vidIQ read it as a "digital planner/sheet" with an "Apple Pencil (or stylus)" **[unclear whether it is a tablet or paper]**.
- **Pointer:** a real hand with a pen or stylus comes in from the right edge and **points** at each section as the voice-over reaches it. It does not write. The page content is already drawn.
- **Page typography:** hand-lettered marker-style titles in black (`THE PAYCHECK RULE` in block caps inside a drawn banner; `That Changed My Finances` in script; `How Long Will it Take to Save` in mixed-case print). Key numbers sit in **highlighter boxes** (`$100,000` in blue or yellow, `$25` and `$50K` in yellow). Step numbers are circled in blue (① ② ③). Formulas are small and grey, with the **result in a green highlighter box**: `$25/hr x 40 hrs/week = $1,000/week`, `(full-time, gross income)`. There are small doodle icons (a cash stack, a bank, a house, two cartoon people, a paycheck note) and margin notes in blue handwriting.
- **Colour coding (paycheck sheets):** cyan for NEEDS, green for INVEST/SAVE, yellow for WANTS, purple or blue for DEBT. The footer is a one-line summary (`60% LIVE • 25% INVEST • 15% ENJOY`).
- **Overlay caption:** one black rounded-rectangle bar with **white bold sans-serif**, roughly centred vertically at 45 to 55% height and spanning about 70 to 80% of the width. Examples: `$25/HR → $100K`, `$20/HR → $100K?! 🤯`, `4 BANK ACCOUNTS = LESS MONEY FIGHTS`, or a 3-line sticker `Paycheck rules / That every family / Should follow`. In the 739K reel the sticker shows only for the first ~1.5 s.
- **Two reveal styles:** (i) the **complete sheet** is visible from frame 1 (the 739K reel and the debt sequel); (ii) a **progressive** page where only the title and step ① are drawn and the rest of the ruled page is blank on the cover (the "How Long Will it Take…" series and the couple reel). **[inferred]** Later sections appear as the video goes on; this was not watched.
- **Audio:** voice-over in the first person ("So here's the paycheck rule that changed my life…") over a soft, low lo-fi or ambient bed. No burned-in auto-captions in the 739K reel.
- **Length:** 37 to 77 s. Cuts: 0 (739K reel, watched).

### Format A': "Static cheat-sheet loop" (5 to 6 s; 2 of 12)
- The same sketchnote notebook sheet fills the frame (spiral on the left edge, black surround). There is no hand, no voice and no overlay bar. Instrumental acoustic or folk guitar plays. Every number is on screen at once. It loops.
- The sheet is very dense (10+ numbers, 3 or 4 columns per rule, "⭐ Best for:" callouts), more than anyone can read in 5.6 s, so viewers have to pause, replay or save.

### Format B: "Armchair talking head + numbered list" (3 of 12)
- **Framing:** medium shot of Yannick, centred in the lower half: a Black man in a baseball cap (a black cap with a white script logo, or a vintage "Fresh Prince"-style cap) and a white or light-blue tee, sitting in a tan or brown leather armchair. Beige wall, light drop ceiling. A desk with a spiral notepad sometimes shows in the foreground.
- **Text stack (top to bottom):**
  1. A **white rounded card with black bold sans-serif** hook at 15 to 25% from the top: `Do all 5 and watch your finance change`.
  2. An **empty numbered list** in large white bold sans-serif with a dark shadow, left-aligned in the upper-left or middle: `1: 2: 3: 4: 5:` or `1. 2. 3. 4.`.
  3. A **black pill with white bold sans-serif** just above the lower third: `They Should've Taught This in School` or `* $20/HR → $10K SAVED`.
  4. **Auto-captions** at the bottom: uppercase bold black text on a white pill, e.g. `NO. 1 TAKE YOUR MONTHLY INCOME` (the 115.8K reel).
- **Number device:** typed digital text next to each numeral. First the **formula** (`5,000 X 200`), then it is **replaced by the result** (`$1M`). Units are spelled out in the result (`$1,040/Month`).
- **Props:** a thick stack of $100 bills waved and pointed (115.8K), or a pen used like a teacher's pointer (50.2K).
- **Audio:** direct speech to camera with a subtle low beat. No pops or dings were reported.
- **Length:** 33 to 38 s. Cuts: 0 (both watched).

## 5. What this channel teaches about look, hooks and formats (this benchmark only)

**Look**
- One continuous shot with no cuts, kept alive by a pointer (a pen or hand) and by text that pops in and swaps. This is cheap to make and leaves plenty of time to read.
- A two-layer text system: a **page or scene layer** (the sheet, or the empty list) and **one high-contrast pill** (black with white bold sans-serif) carrying the hook or the "$IN → $OUT" claim at mid-height.
- Results are emphasised with **highlighter boxes** (green for results, yellow or blue for the key input and goal); formulas are small and grey. On camera, the formula line is **replaced** by its result.
- Colour coding is consistent across sheets: cyan for needs, green for invest, yellow for wants, purple or blue for debt.
- The page is not a kraft envelope or a collage. It is a clean, white, lined notebook sheet with tidy marker lettering and pastel highlights.

**Hooks**
- Lead with the **input and promised output** ($20/HR → $10K SAVED), or with an **identity group plus a rule** ("every family should follow").
- Make the open loop **visible**: empty numbered slots, or a mostly blank page with only step ① filled.
- Say the first calculation in the first sentence ("Number one: take your monthly income, multiply it by 200").
- Either put the biggest number first (the $1M at about 2 to 8 s) or hold it to the end as the payoff for the badge (the $10K at 31 to 36 s). Both happen here.

**Formats**
- **Walkthrough, 33 to 61 s:** one anchor input (a $5,000 month or a $20 to $30 hour), 4 or 5 formula lines, and a final goal number.
- **6 s cheat-sheet loop:** gets high play counts but very low likes and comments. It is cheap reach, not engagement.
- **Series by input swap:** the same template rerun at $20, $25 or $30 an hour and $10K, $50K or $100K goals.
