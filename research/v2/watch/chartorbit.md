## 2. The ChartOrbit look (synthesis across the 4 watched videos)

**Frame layout (9:16), house template (#1-#3)**
- **Top ~25%: a fixed header on white.** It stacks three things:
  - **The premise line:** one sentence in black sans, medium to bold weight, centred. Examples: "POV: In 2002 You invested $5000 in", and "POV: Since 1996 you / invested $100/month in / and never sold" (in a white rounded box with a soft border).
  - **The stake, the only accent:** "$5000" in green (#2E7D32 and #22C55E reported), or "$100/month" underlined in teal/blue.
  - **The contenders:** brand marks or flags with "VS" between them, for example the red NETFLIX wordmark "VS" the black Disney script logo, or the US flag "VS" the EU flag. The single-stock series shows one logo (the BMW roundel, top right).
- **Credits row under the header:** "Source: Yahoo Finance" on the left and "Creator: Data Insights" on the right, in small regular sans, grey or black.
- **Bottom ~75%: the chart.**
  - The year counter is huge, bold and black, at the top left of the plot ("2002", "2008", "1997").
  - The grid is light grey. The y-axis is on the left in $K, then $M ("3.2K-5.0K" growing to millions), and it **auto-rescales** as values grow. The x-axis shows repeated year ticks.
  - Each line head carries a two-line live label, name then value, in the line's colour: "Netflix / $ 4,043", "S&P500 / $ 4,676", "Investment / $ 300", "Dividends / $ 0".
  - The watermark "@data_insights" sits faint at the lower left of the grid.
- **Event bands:** translucent pink, salmon or coral vertical bands slide in for crashes. Each has a bold black label at the top of the chart: "Financial Crisis", "Dot-com Bust", "Global Fin. Crisis", "Eurozone Debt Shock", "COVID Crash", "2022 Inflation Fear", "Tariff Shock", "Major Drawdown 1998". They do the job of captions and narration.

**Variant (#4, NVIDIA DCA, 14.1x):** three tiers.
- **Top ~25%:** black textured panel with white sans "Since 1999 you invested $100/month in" (no "POV:"), "$100/month" in light green, a Jensen Huang cut-out on the left, the green NVIDIA logo in the centre and a GPU photo on the right.
- **Middle ~50%:** the usual white chart.
- **Bottom ~25%:** black meme panel with Leonardo DiCaprio cheering (The Wolf of Wall Street), a glowing candlestick chart and Mr. Krabs lifting money.

**Palette:** pure white #FFFFFF background, black type, and brand colours for the lines. Reported colours:
- Netflix red #E50914 vs Disney black.
- S&P500 deep crimson #8B1E2D vs EUROSTOXX50 royal blue #1E3A8A.
- BMW steel blue #2B7A9B, Investment slate grey #708090, Dividends forest green #1E7E34.
- NVIDIA lime #76B900.

There is one green accent for the stake, and the crash bands are pink or coral (#FF8A8A reported).

**Type:** one clean geometric sans (the watcher compares it to Inter or Helvetica). Bold for the year counter, the line values and the header. Regular for axis ticks and credits. Brand logos keep their own lettering (Disney script).

**How numbers appear:**
- **Live odometer counters** at each line tip, in whole dollars with comma separators ("$ 4,811,008").
- An **auto-scaling axis**: the numbers on the axis go from K to M, which makes compounding look explosive.
- A **big ticking year counter**.
- In the DCA series, a **cost-basis line** ("Investment") and a **dividends line**, so what you put in, what it is worth and what it paid are three racing numbers.

There is never handwriting, a calculator, a table, a formula or a narrator.

**Motion:** one smooth, continuous left-to-right time-lapse at a steady pace of roughly 2-2.7 s per year (check: 2002 to 2025 is 23 years in 61 s, about 2.7 s/yr; 1996 to 2025 is 29 years in 60 s, about 2.1 s/yr). There are 0 cuts. The only "events" are the axis rescaling and the crash bands sliding in.

**Audio:** ABBA "Money, Money, Money" (#1-#4; #3 is described as an "upbeat remix cover"). An instrumental or synth intro runs to about 0:11, then the vocals start ("I work all night, I work all day to pay the bills…" in #3). There are no SFX.

**Length and ending:** 60-61 s. Each short ends abruptly on the 2025 values, with no CTA and no end card. No loop seam was designed. The replay simply restarts at the opening crash.

## 5. What this means for Back of the Envelope (from this channel only)

- **Put the whole premise in frame 1, and keep it there.** That means one second-person sentence ("you put $X in…"), the stake as the only coloured word, and named contenders shown as logos or flags. The premise is a fixed header, not a title card that cuts away.
- **Start in motion and in the red.** The first visible numbers are already below the stake. The tension comes before the payoff.
- **Show "money in" next to "money now".** The grey Investment line plus a live counter at each line tip is the closest this channel gets to showing working, and it reads with no narration. That is the gap BotE can fill: rough-but-right working beside the race.
- **Back-load the biggest number** (the last 5-8 s), with a first payoff between 0:14 and 0:47 of a 60 s video, and end dead on the final value so the replay starts on the crash.
- **Choose pairs for rivalry and a lopsided result.** Household brands and countries win; same-sector twins and indices sit at the bottom of the series. Country pairs draw about 4x the comments per view.
