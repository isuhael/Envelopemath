- **Top 0 to ~14%: an empty black band.** No text sits there. It is visible on the covers of #1, #2 and #7. It reads as a safe zone for IG's top UI.
- **~14% to ~85%: a full-width dark charcoal infographic panel.** The headline sits at the top of the panel, centred. The grid runs below it and is anchored on the left.
- **Lower right, from ~40% down: the host.** He is green-screened (walkthrough: "Green screen overlay") and covers the right-hand part of the lower rows.
  - In the ETF reel he points up at the grid with his index finger.
  - In the crash reel he covers the lower two DCA cells, then **shifts his body at 0:34 to reveal them** (walkthrough). His body works as a masking device.
- **Wardrobe:** a black cap, with a grey t-shirt (ETF reel, cover) or a cream t-shirt (#7). In the crash reel there is no cap and he wears an olive hoodie with a white Nike swoosh.

## 7. What transfers to Back of the Envelope (from this benchmark only)

1. **Make frame 1 a finished data sheet, not a teaser.** Both breakouts put the whole grid of answers on screen at 0.0 s under a 3-line typeset headline, and the viewer stays to read it. For a rough-but-right channel this is a strong fit: show the envelope's full working as a grid, then walk it.
2. **"Dollarize the percentage" is a ready-made envelope hook.** "What you're buying when you invest $10,000 in ..." turns percentages into dollar amounts of a round sum, with a remainder line ("The remaining $7,770 is split between 496 other companies"). The working is one multiplication and one subtraction per row.
3. **Pair a red worst case with a cyan relief number in two columns.** "How long it took to recover..." puts 25 YEARS in red beside ~6.7 YEARS in cyan. The colour carries the verdict before a word is read. Hide or mask the relief column, then reveal it about halfway (he does it with his body at 0:34; a faceless version could use a mask or wipe).
4. **Use pop-in arrows with a pop SFX to sync the eye to the number being spoken.** It is the only motion on an otherwise static graphic, and it is cheap.
5. **Design system, in concrete terms:**
   - **Canvas:** a dark charcoal or near-black panel, with an empty top band of about 14% for platform UI.
   - **Headline:** heavy white geometric sans, title case, 3 lines, ending in a colon.
   - **Numbers:** bold, one meaning per colour (red bad, cyan good, yellow column heads, a rainbow per row for rankings).
   - **Row anchors:** recognisable logos or mini-charts.
6. **Skip the red sticker as the hook.** On this account it appears only on the non-breakouts (21K to 67K). The breakouts use the graphic's own typeset headline.
7. **A 5 s calendar ladder is a cheap filler format.** "You've got 93 days left in 2026." followed by "$X each day = $Y" reaches about the account median with no host and no voice. The caption carries the second step (×1.1^15, written as "~$").
8. **End on a rule.** Both breakouts end on a behaviour rule ("check the top 10 holdings"; "invest every time we get paid"). A keyword-comment CTA lifts comments (617; 199) but did not lift plays on #10.
