## 2. Analyst notes (my own analysis)

**Math check.** 55 + 5 + 10 + 15 + 15 = 100%. $1,925 + $175 + $350 + $525 + $525 = $3,500. Internally correct. The framework allocates **40% to future-you** (10 + 15 + 15) and 60% to present-you (55 + 5).

**Title/overlay mismatch:** the YouTube title promises "How I Split Every Paycheck (The Exact 5 Percentages)", but the on-screen header says "How to be financially free 💸 (in 5 steps)". The words "paycheck" and "monthly income" are used interchangeably, although for biweekly earners a paycheck isn't the same as monthly income. A pedant could comment on that. Whether it was intentional is unknown.

**Formula anatomy:**
1. **Zero-preamble hook.** The first sentence is the first instruction, so nothing is wasted before the value starts.
2. **A repeating sentence template** ("Take your monthly income and multiply it by X") makes it rhythmic and predictable, so viewers anticipate the next number.
3. **Formula → result morph.** Seeing `$3,500 * .55` turn into `$1925/m` is a tiny "calculator moment" five times over.
4. **A numbered open loop** (1 of 5) keeps people watching to the end.
5. **A save CTA** matched to a save-worthy format.

**This is literally envelope budgeting.** Splitting a paycheck into 5 fixed buckets is the cash-envelope / #cashstuffing method without the envelopes. This is the most on-brand video in group 4 for "Back of the Envelope". We can make the double meaning (envelope math + cash envelopes) explicit.

**How Back of the Envelope can beat it (ideas for the teaser step, not final scripts):**
1. **Five physical or illustrated envelopes, labelled on screen** (ESSENTIALS 55 / FUN 5 / DEBT 10 / SHORT-TERM 15 / LONG-TERM 15). Each fills as the voiceover hits it. This fixes vidIQ's main critique, since the labels persist and are screenshot-able, which helps saves.
2. **Teach the mental shortcut, not the calculator.** This is our core differentiator: "rough but right" in your head.
   - 10% = move the decimal: $3,500 → **$350**
   - 5% = half of that → **$175**
   - 15% = 10% + 5% → **$525**
   - 55% = half plus 5% → $1,750 + $175 = **$1,925**
   The viewer leaves able to split *any* paycheck without a phone, which goes beyond the creator's version.
3. **"Comment your take-home and we'll envelope it."** This turns the cost-of-living debate into a reply-video series (comment-reply shorts are a cheap follow-up format).
4. **Loop it.** End on the five full envelopes being sealed and the first envelope opening again.
5. **Add one envelope-math twist at the end** to give it a payoff: "15% invested monthly at $3,500 is $6,300 a year". Then show one compounding projection, clearly labelled with an assumed return. (Compute and verify the figures in the teaser step; none are given here.)
