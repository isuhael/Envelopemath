## My analysis: Envelope Math takeaways
- **Format archetype:** "Pop-culture price tag." Take a famous fictional or celebrity setting and put a real-world dollar figure on it. The *question* is the hook. The number is the payoff.
- **The math gap is our opening.** The source is pure expert assertion. Envelope Math can **show the envelope**: comps per square foot x estimated square footage, the 40x income rule, and how many years of a Central Perk barista's tips that is. Rough but right, worked out on screen in handwriting.
- **Steal:** (a) the hook question as title, overlay and first spoken line all at once; (b) a scorecard/progress structure that delays the number to about 90% of runtime; (c) a named sequel CTA ("Joey and Chandler's next").
- **Avoid or fix:** relying on licensed TV clips (copyright and Content ID risk for a faceless channel). Use stylised envelope-sketch recreations of iconic rooms instead.
- **Loop idea:** end on "...which is why Monica's real rent was..." and cut straight back to the "$200" anchor at frame 1.
