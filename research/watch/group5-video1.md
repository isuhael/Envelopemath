## My analysis: Envelope Math takeaways
- **Why such a high outlier (1228x) with almost no production?** My inference, not verified: the channel's baseline is tiny (a local professional-services account), so a modest absolute view count produces a huge ratio. The topic itself, "the minimum payment is a trap", is a known evergreen finance theme. The takeaway is that **the topic carried this video, not the craft.** A better-produced version of the same idea has a lot of room to grow.
- **Our whitespace is the math the video skips.** "It takes forever" begs to be quantified. My own illustrative calculations (assumptions stated; card terms vary by issuer and country):
  - $5,000 at 22% APR, minimum = interest + 1% of balance (floor $25): about **230 months (~19 years)**, about **$13,100** paid in total.
  - $5,000 at 20.99% APR, minimum = 3% of balance (floor $10): about **266 months (~22 years)**, about **$11,700** paid in total.
  - Envelope rule of thumb: when the minimum barely exceeds the monthly interest (APR/12), the principal hardly moves. That is the whole trick in one line.
- **Format to steal:** the myth-bust opener ("If you're paying the minimum, you're...") said in the first second. Unlike this video, we put it **on screen** as well as in the voiceover.
- **Envelope twist:** show a literal envelope labelled "MINIMUM PAYMENT". Each month a bill goes in, and an animated split shows most of it going to "INTEREST" and a sliver to "DEBT". A counter ticks up years, ending on "Year 19. Still paying." Loop that line back to the first frame.
- **Avoid:** giving personalised debt or insolvency advice. Keep it to illustrative math with stated assumptions, and say that terms vary.
