## My analysis: Envelope Math takeaways
- **Format archetype:** "How much does [famous thing] make?" Creator and brand economics. The title's specific dollar figure ($11M/mo) is the click driver, even though the video only says "millions".
- **Our gap: derive the number.** Our version opens with the same question and then **does the envelope**: views per month x RPM ÷ 1,000 = ad revenue, with the RPM range stated as an assumption and the result shown as a range ("$X–$Y a month"). That answers what the title promises and gives commenters something concrete to argue about (whether the RPM is too high or too low).
- **Steal:** (a) the sketch-animation look (pencil hatching on paper), which maps directly onto handwriting on an envelope; (b) escalating number reveals (2.5B, then 67M, then 100M, then $120M, then $3B), each a step up the ladder; (c) the "comment if you want part 2" CTA.
- **Improve:** keep it under 60 s (this runs 2:19; it is a single biography beat stretched out); put the derived monthly number in the first 3 s; loop the ending ("...and nobody knows who they are" back to the opening).
- **Accuracy caution:** the video itself hedges the sale price ("Nobody knows exactly how much for"). Any Envelope Math version of a creator-income story must label estimates as estimates and cite sources for any reported deal figures.
